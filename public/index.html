Then and now

The same wonders, a different kind of journey

In 1912, Hiram Bingham's Yale expedition camped at Machu Picchu for months, clearing and photographing the ruins. When those pictures ran in National Geographic in 1913, much of the world saw the place for the first time.

Today a train follows the Urubamba River to the foot of the mountain, and you can book your ticket from your phone. The stones, the clouds and that first look over the terraces have not changed. That is the feeling these guides are written for.
