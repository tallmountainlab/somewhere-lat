--- title: About somewhere.lat description: Who we are and how we research our Latin America travel guides. ---

Remember flipping through a travel magazine and stopping at a photo of Machu Picchu in the clouds, or a glacier in Patagonia, and thinking "someday"? somewhere.lat is for that someday. We write guides to the great trips of Latin America, the ones people have dreamed about for generations, and make them simple to plan.

Every place we cover has a story that goes back a long way, and we like to tell a little of it. Travel is more meaningful when you know what came before you.

How we write our guides

Every guide mixes a little history and a lot of practical advice.

  • We lead with what you need to decide and book, then the highlights.
  • Every guide shows the date it was last reviewed, and we revisit guides regularly.
  • Tickets, park rules and entry requirements change, so we link to official sources and ask you to confirm before you book.

Share your memories

Do you have an old photo, a postcard or a story from one of the places we write about? We would love to hear it, and with your permission we may share it in a guide. Send it to hello@somewhere.lat.

Spotted something out of date? Email us at hello@somewhere.lat.
